🏆 WORLD CUP FORMAT – OFFICIAL TOURNAMENT RULES

1️⃣ Tournament Format

  • Competition follows a World Cup Format Edition.
  • Players are arranged into groups.
  • Group stage is played first.
  • Top teams advance to the knockout stage.
  • Round of 16 is played over two legs.
  • Quarter-final, Semi-final, and Final are single-leg matches.

2️⃣ Match Settings

  • Match duration: 10 minutes
  • Condition: EX / EX
  • Maximum of 2 matches per day per player

3️⃣ Official Match Time Window

  • Each matchday starts at 10:00 AM and ends at 10:00 AM the following day.
  • All matches must be played within this time frame.
  • Any match played outside the official time window:
    • Will NOT be recorded.
    • May be recorded as 0–0 (nil-nil).
  • No exceptions will be made.

4️⃣ Points System (Group Stage)

  • Win = 3 points
  • Draw = 1 point
  • Loss = 0 points

If teams are tied on points:

  1. Goal Difference (GD)
  2. Goals Scored
  3. Admin decision (if still tied)

No goal limit. All goals count.

5️⃣ Knockout Stage Rules

🔹 Round of 16 (Two Legs)

  • Played over two matches.
  • If aggregate score is tied:
    • A straight 60-minute rematch will be played.
    • No extra time.
    • Penalty shootout will decide the winner.

🔹 Quarter-Final, Semi-Final & Final (Single Leg)

  • Full match must be played.
  • Extra time is allowed (30 minutes).
  • If still tied after extra time:
    • Penalty shootout will decide the winner.

6️⃣ Walkover Rule (No Show)

  • If a player does not show up, opponent must provide evidence via DM chat only.
  • Public tagging as evidence is NOT allowed.
  • Official walkover score: 3–0.
  • Admin decision is final.

7️⃣ Result Submission

  • Screenshot must be taken after each match.
  • Screenshot must be sent to admin.
  • Player must tag admin with appropriate caption.
  • Results posted after deadline will NOT be recorded.
  • Saying “I didn’t know the deadline” will not be accepted.

8️⃣ Disconnection Rules

  • 20–30 mins → Replay 8 minutes
  • 30–40 mins → Replay 7 minutes
  • 40–45 mins → Replay 5 minutes
  • 45–70 mins → Replay 6 minutes
  • 70–80 mins → Replay first half of 5 minutes
  • If cancellation occurs from 80 minutes by the losing party:
    • The current score stands as final
    • Only if the winning player is leading by at least 2 goals
  • Admin decision is final in disputed cases.

9️⃣ Discipline & Conduct

  • No violence.
  • No unnecessary arguments.
  • Respect your opponent.
  • Insulting or abusive behavior = 1 match suspension.
  • Repeated misconduct may lead to disqualification.
  • Suspended player may be reinstated only at admin discretion.

🔟 Website & Administration Rules

  • Only the admin can enter official scores into the system.
  • Once the next stage is generated, previous stage results cannot be changed.
  • Stage progression is controlled strictly by admin.
  • Any dispute must be resolved before stage generation.
  • Admin decision is final in all matters.

✅ For enjoyment
✅ For fair play
